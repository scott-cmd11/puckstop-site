PuckStop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026
🛡️ Data Not Collected

PuckStop is an offline-first hockey goalie stat tracker. It is built so that everything you record stays on your device.

The short version

No accounts. No cloud. No analytics. No tracking. No ads. Your goalie's stats never leave your device unless you choose to export them yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. PuckStop does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We have no servers and no analytics, so we never see your data.

Where your data lives

All goalies, games, and shot events are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database. Data is included in your normal encrypted device backups (iCloud or Finder) if you have those enabled — that is handled by Apple, not by us.

Exporting & sharing

PuckStop lets you export your data as CSV or a JSON backup, and share a game "stat card" image. These actions are initiated by you, and the resulting file or image goes only where you send it.

Children's privacy

PuckStop is designed for parents and coaches tracking youth hockey goalies. Because the app collects no data and requires no account, it does not knowingly gather any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
