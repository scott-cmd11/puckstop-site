Help for the next game.

PuckStop is designed for a cold rink, a fast game, and a parent or coach who needs the next action to be obvious.

Quick answers

How do I record a normal shot?

Use Save when the goalie stops the shot and Goal when the shot goes in. Add the shot origin when you have time; the live record should not make you miss the next play.

How do I fix a mistake?

Open the event from the game record, edit the detail that is wrong, or undo the most recent event. Review the totals before you close the game.

How do I handle an empty-net goal?

Use the separate Empty Net action. It stays in the game result without changing normal goalie shots, saves, save percentage, or regulation GAA. Read the full explanation →

How do I export my stats?

Create a PDF/CSV Stats Package or a JSON backup, then choose where to send it in Apple’s share sheet. The developer does not receive an automatic copy.

Does PuckStop need an account?

No account is required for the core app. Stats are stored on your device. See the privacy policy for the optional calendar-link request.

Need a hand?

Email scott.hazlitt@gmail.com with the app version, device model, what you expected, and what happened. Please do not include a child’s private information or a full stats export unless support asks for it.
